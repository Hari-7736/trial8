Common questions

Answers before you pick up the phone.

Which businesses need GST registration?

Registration depends on your annual turnover, the kind of goods or services you supply and whether you sell across state lines or online. We check your position against the current rules and register you if required.

Can you handle income tax or GST notices?

Yes. Send us the notice as soon as it arrives. We review it, prepare the reply and represent you where needed.

How are your fees decided?

Fees depend on the scope of work, the volume of transactions and the filing frequency. We agree a fixed fee in writing before we begin.

Do you work with clients outside your city?

Yes. Most documents are shared securely online and we meet over video or phone, so location is rarely a limitation.

What should I bring to the first consultation?

Bring your PAN, any GST registration details, recent bank statements and last year's returns if you have them. If you are starting out, just bring your questions.
